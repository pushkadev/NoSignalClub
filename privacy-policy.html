Privacy Policy
NoSignalClub

Last updated: March 2026  ·  Effective: March 2026

Overview

NoSignalClub ("the App") is a personal utility that forwards WhatsApp notifications to an SMS number of your choice — designed for use with feature phones that cannot run WhatsApp.

This policy explains what data the App accesses, how it is used, and confirms that no personal data is collected, stored, or transmitted to any server or third party.

Permissions & Why We Need Them

  • SEND_SMS
    Send SMS Messages Used to forward incoming WhatsApp notification content to the phone number you configure in Settings. SMS messages are sent directly from your device using your own SIM card and carrier.
  • NOTIF
    Notification Listener Access Required to read the content of incoming WhatsApp notifications. This is the only way to intercept messages without modifying WhatsApp itself. The App reads notification text solely to construct the SMS to be forwarded.
  • BATTERY
    Ignore Battery Optimization (optional) Allows the background service to remain active. Without this, Android may kill the service to save battery. This permission is optional and does not affect any data.
All processing happens entirely on your device. Notification content is never logged, stored in a database, or sent anywhere other than the SMS number you explicitly configure.

Data Collection

The App does not collect any personal data. Specifically:

  • NO
    No analytics or tracking The App contains no analytics SDK, crash reporter, or tracking library of any kind.
  • NO
    No cloud storage No data is uploaded to any server. There is no backend. The App has no internet permission.
  • NO
    No accounts or registration The App does not require sign-in, account creation, or any form of identification.

Local Storage

The App stores only two values locally on your device using Android's DataStore API:

  • KEY
    Target phone number The SMS destination number you enter in Settings.
  • KEY
    Enabled / disabled state Whether the forwarding service is currently active.
This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing App data in Android Settings.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party SDKs, advertising networks, payment processors, or external APIs. It is entirely self-contained.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. Since no data is collected from any user, this policy applies equally regardless of age.
